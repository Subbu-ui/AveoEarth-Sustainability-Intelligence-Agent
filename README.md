# AveoEarth Sustainability & Funding Intelligence Agent

## Project Overview

This project is an Agentic AI-based sustainability and funding intelligence system developed for AveoEarth.

The objective of the system is to reduce the manual effort required to continuously search, analyse and monitor:

- European university-linked funding opportunities
- Europe sustainability developments
- India sustainability schemes and business opportunities
- Changes in previously identified opportunities

The system converts scattered web information into structured and prioritised business intelligence.

---

## Business Problem

Sustainability-related information is spread across:

- university websites
- government portals
- policy publications
- sustainability reports
- funding pages
- MSME and artisan-related schemes

Manually reviewing these sources repeatedly requires significant time and may result in missed or outdated opportunities.

The system automates a large part of this research workflow.
